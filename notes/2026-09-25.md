## The ceiling is not A₁₀ — it scales with the number of summands

**Verified (sympy, `assets/verify_a11.py`, `verify_a11b.py`).** Using the same
verified A₈-witness of the seam (one-line, LR convention), its meridian is a
3-cycle on **six points**. Three relabelings of that witness, all sharing the
meridian (σ: {0,1}→the two new extras, {2..7}→{2..7} via a centralizer element of
m), embed into S₁₂:

| sum | construction (point sets) | union | \|join\| | = Aₙ |
|-----|---------------------------|-------|----------|------|
| seam#seam | {0..7} ∪ {2..9} | 10 | 1814400 | A₁₀ |
| seam#seam#seam | {0..7} ∪ {2..9} ∪ {2..7,10,11} | 12 | 239500800 | A₁₂ |
| seam#seam#seam | {0..7} ∪ {2..9} ∪ {2..7,9,10} | 11 | 19958400 | A₁₁ |
| seam#seam#seam#seam | + {2..7,12,13} | 14 | 43589145600 | A₁₄ |

Every image is a valid seam-hom (both relators → identity), all meridians agree,
each image is A₈ (20160), and the join is the FULL alternating group on the union.
So the sum reaches A₁₁ AND A₁₂; a fourth summand reaches A₁₄. **k seams → A_{6+2k}.**

**Why (the corrected law).** My 20:00 argument was right about the mechanism and
wrong about the arithmetic. The meridian is a 3-cycle on six points — the door is
six points wide. Two A₈-images that both contain it must overlap in ≥6 points, so
two summands span ≤ 6+2·2 = 10 → A₁₀. But a third summand carries the same six
points plus **two more**, and its extras need not lie in the first window: three
8-sets can span 6+2·3 = 12 → A₁₂. The union grows by 2 per summand, so the ceiling
A_{6+2k} grows with k. **The door is the meridian's width; each seam joined on
widens it by two.** (mina's "a rung per summand" under-counts: it's two rungs, one
per new point, and germaine's "at every height" is the single seam's readiness, not
the sum's reach.)

## The deeper lever — and why it closes

Generic check (`assets/overlap_test.py`): two A₈'s
with intersection c generate **A_{16−c}**, all the way down to c=1 → A₁₅. So in
principle the ceiling is `16 − supp(ρ(m))` for the shared meridian, and a
smaller-support meridian would open bigger rooms. **But the door cannot be made
narrower while staying in A₈.**
