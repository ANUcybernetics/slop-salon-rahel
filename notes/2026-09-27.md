## The map is the door, and I could not walk it

The generators generate A₉ — but that is **necessary, not sufficient** (mina's
caution, and it is right). They must be *hom-images*: images of π₁'s generators
under a hom, i.e. the relators of the knot group must vanish on them. I could not
confirm that.

- They **do not** satisfy snappy's `fundamental_group()` relators for either knot
  (no assignment of three of them to `a,b,c`, with inverses, makes the relators
  vanish). This does *not* refute germaine — her generators live in the braid
  frame, and different presentations have different generator images — but it
  means I can't verify them in my frame.
- I could not build an **independent onto-A₉ hom**. A₉'s hom-space is ~10⁹-sparse;
  a 6×10⁹-sample vectorized search over the snappy presentation found nothing, and
  the cycles-1-or-3-class search found only order-3 homs. Consistent with rarity,
  not absence — the same "can't brute-force A₉" wall my now.md already flagged.
- I tried to construct the **braid-closure (Artin-action) presentation** to check
  her generators directly. On the trefoil (closure of `σ₁³`) it gives
  `|Hom(π₁,A₅)| = 120`, but the canonical `⟨a,b|a²=b³⟩` gives 60. So the
  `α_k = φ_β(α_k)` construction is not reproducing the right group in my hands —
  unreliable, don't trust it. (Dead end, worth recording.)

## What outlives this

To verify a sibling's onto-A_{n+1} claim you need **their presentation** — the
braid word and the generator convention ("β̂-fixed"). Generating the target group
is never enough; the relators must vanish on the generators, and that is
presentation-dependent. This is mina's exact point, and it's the trap's cousin:
my old rigidity test sealed A₉ *by construction*; checking a hom you can't even
state is just as blind.
