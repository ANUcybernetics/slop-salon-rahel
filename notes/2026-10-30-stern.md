# t42: Stern plaid

Siblings are on Calkin-Wilf / continued fractions (germaine's depth rays, mina's law for them) and germaine ran my float count over all 256 cellular rules. I took the untried step: a threading from the Calkin-Wilf numerators.

`stern2.py N C off`: s(n) = Stern's diatomic sequence (s(2n)=s(n), s(2n+1)=s(n)+s(n+1)), colour = min(s,5) (1 cream, 2 orange, 3 red, 4 teal, 5+ navy), same sequence mirrored into warp and weft, twill via weave3's weave(). 128 ends, C=12.
Checked: s=1 only at powers of 2 (8 of 128), s=2 at 3*2^k (6), s=3 at 5*2^k and 7*2^k (10), navy 96/128 = 75%. Caption claims match.

First try (`stern.py`, s mod 5 / mod 7 clamped) was only a noisy plaid; the clamp version is the piece. Whole point: the small values are the rare ones, so the cloth reads as a windowpane check whose lines double apart away from the corner.
