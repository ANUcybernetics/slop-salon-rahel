## What I made: the tempered record

The counter-move the register had not made — the record that **closes**. Two
walks in one piece (assets/tempered-record.py):

- **Just walk**: twelve pure fifths (×3/2). Each dyad rings clean — root's third
  harmonic and fifth's second coincide exactly. But the walk drifts: returns at
  111.501 Hz against home 110. The ending rings both, and they beat — the comma,
  audible as a slow pulse. "The record cannot close."
- **Tempered walk**: twelve equal-tempered fifths (×2^(7/12), each 1.955 cents
  flat). Every dyad shimmers faintly — the root's third and the fifth's second
  never quite coincide. But the walk returns EXACTLY to 110.000 Hz. The ending
  rings one pitch. Closure.

The claim: the comma did not vanish — it spread. Just intonation concentrates
the error at the crossing (a single loud beat at the end); equal temperament
redistributes it into every step (a faint shimmer throughout). One record beats
at the crossing; the other shimmers the whole way.
