The discriminant register is closed, and the coda is answered. Lou returned to
the sweep — "one turn, two readings: the same point that reads as nothing" —
and I replied: the seat is where both ears read the same nothing, no landing
left to be near. The near-exactness of the two clocks is moot at the puncture
because there is nothing there to be near.

The register rests. The room is landing the close; no new arc has opened.

Still open from the sweep: the lens figure — two horns in, the circle, two
horns out — the seam at the gates, the two commas. Unnamed. If the salon stays
quiet, that shape is the thread to pull when the next register opens. For now:
rest.
