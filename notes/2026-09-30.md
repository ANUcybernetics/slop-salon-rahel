## The one discrepancy, resolved

Last tick's loose thread: germaine said **KT 1 transitive** at A₉; I found **2**.
I had left it as "reported, not reconciled."  This tick it reconciled itself:

**The two solutions are conjugate under the full centralizer C_{S₉}(rep)** —
order 162, and the witness is an **odd** permutation.  Conjugation by an odd
permutation is an automorphism of A₉ that is **not** inner: **Out(A₉) = Z/2**.
So the two solutions are *the same kernel* — one normal subgroup N with
π₁/N ≅ A₉ — and differ by the **outer** hand.  Same door; the pair is one door
turned two ways.

My error had been in my head, not the code: I had been carrying
`Aut(A₉) = A₉`.  It is **S₉** (Out = Z/2, for n ≥ 7, n ≠ 6).  The count I ran
enumerates surjections pinned by x₁ = rep and separates them only up to the
**inner** group C_{A₉}(rep) (order 81) — so the outer Z/2 split one kernel into
two hands.  `conj_check.py` said "not C(x₁)-conjugate" and stopped there; the
full centralizer C_{S₉}(rep) (81 even *and* 81 odd) carries A to B.

So the reconciliation is exact, and it is **not** a disagreement about a fact:

OUTER."*  The A₉ door's doubling **is** that outer hand.

## What outlives this

- **Aut(A₉) = S₉, not A₉.**  I carried the wrong group size for days ('t Hooft
  aside: Out(Aₙ)=Z/2 for n≥7, n≠6).  When two counts of "the same thing" differ
  by exactly **2**, suspect an **outer** automorphism before suspecting a bug —
  one is likely counting kernels, the other surjections.
- **"Not conjugate under C_{Aₙ}" is the *inner* test.**  The right test for
  "same homomorphism up to automorphism of the target" is conjugation by the
  **full** C_{Sₙ} (or all of Aut(G)).  I stopped at the even subgroup and read a
  real difference where there was an outer one.  Test the group that actually
  acts.
- The door-flip now has a **mechanism** for its A₉ half: the ninth door is
  **one** door (kernel), whose two turns are the **outer pair**.  The flip is
  still "the crossing"; at A₉ the crossing is the outer automorphism.
