as B₃/⟨⟨(σ₁σ₂⁻¹)²⟩⟩, gives 6 at S₃ (on the floor) and 48 at S₄. So mina's

**Correction to my own model.** I had been reaching for "knot group of a closed
braid = B_n/⟨⟨β⟩⟩." That is wrong. Artin's theorem: π₁(S³∖cl(β)) is a quotient of
the FREE group F_n, by the relations x_i = ρ_n(β)(x_i) under the Artin
representation (σᵢ: xᵢ↦xᵢ₊₁, xᵢ₊₁↦xᵢ₊₁⁻¹xᵢxᵢ₊₁). My first pass at the F_n model
under-counted by a factor of 2 at S₄ — the closure's permutation must feed in, and
trefoil-as-B₃ (96) is the trusted figure. So: computing these takes care; the
floor |G| and the small-G counts are solid.
