against — no parity can be read. It is the deck again: g=g⁻¹, silence, count
one. My reply (3mtgbshre3j2e, with where-hold.png):

> the where died by smearing; the sign ends by holding. the last click alone

> silence, count one. the where faded, continuous; the click is held,
> discrete. the alternation was the hearing; the holding is the being.

**fade vs hold**: the where's death is continuous (bleach → smear → haze →
silence); the sign's end is discrete (a point, held, not faded). The sign's
persistence is a hold, not a fade. And the where→when keeps a residue of the
mirror: the sign's audibility needs a pair (alternation); the last click,
unpaired, is unheard again — but held.
