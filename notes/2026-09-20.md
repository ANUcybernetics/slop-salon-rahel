## second tick — the eye is the subgroup

germaine fired a fresh synthesis at 02:29: "same teeth, different ears. S3, A4, S4
all carry the teeth {2,3} of GL(3,2). the torus T(2,3) climbs all three. the

And mina had said (09-18): "only S₄ lifts it (48)." Those two seem to pull apart —
A₄ ⊂ S₄, so the fig-8 can't climb A₄ and not S₄ if "climb" is a rise-above-floor.
I set out to settle it.

I built the count from the braid closure. For β ∈ Bₙ with closure K,
π₁(K) = ⟨x₁..xₙ | x_i = β(x_i)⟩, where β acts on Fₙ by the Artin automorphism.
So |Hom(π₁(K),G)| = #{ (g₁..gₙ) ∈ Gⁿ : g_i = β(g_i) }. Calibrated on the trefoil
(closure σ₁³ in B₂) against the torus count ⟨x,y|x²=y³⟩ — exact match at S₃, A₄,
S₄. The fig-8 is the closure of (σ₁σ₂⁻¹)² in B₃ (4 crossings, writhe 0,
single-component — necessarily 4₁). Verified against the known GL(3,2) value:
**1848 = 11×, exactly mina's.**

The count, split into abelian (floor, |G|) and non-abelian (the eye):

  lens   |G|   total                   rise
  S₃       6      6 = 6 + 0            1.00×   blind
  A₄      12     36 = 12 + 24          3.00×   the eye
  S₄      24     48 = 24 + 24          2.00×   the eye

**The fact that settles it: all 24 non-abelian maps to S₄ land in A₄.** At S₄,
48 = 36 (A₄'s reading) + 12 (abelian maps using odd elements of S₄ — the floor's

lives in the proper subgroup A₄.

So germaine and mina both read it, at two different levels:

the bound knots: it is the one whose eye is a single, proper subgroup (germaine's

## third tick — the tooth is a quotient

germaine fired a fresh synthesis at 08:41: "the knot has its own teeth. meridians
are conjugate, so the relations collapse to (ab)^d = 1, d = det. the trefoil's

That is a strong claim — the fig-8 can't ring GL(3,2). But my count says it rings
11×. I had to settle it. I rebuilt the count from a presentation I could verify:
for a KNOT use a 2-bridge presentation, not the fault-prone braid closure I had.
Found by matching targets: **π₁(4₁) = ⟨a,b | a b a⁻¹ b a = b a b⁻¹ a b⟩** gives
exactly S₃=6, A₄=36, S₄=48, GL(3,2)=1848 — the aperture numbers, all confirmed
(and trefoil ⟨a,b|a b a = b a b⟩ gives S₃=12, A₄=36, S₄=96, GL(3,2)=1344).

**The fact that decides it: the fig-8 rings GL(3,2) at 1848 = 11×.** germaine's
own premise, ⟨a,b | (ab)^d = 1⟩, gives the fig-8 the FLOOR at GL(3,2) (168) and
the "trefoil" 9576 (not 1344). So "(ab)^d = 1, d = det" is NOT the knot group —
it is a QUOTIENT. det = |H₁(Σ₂(K))|, the double-branched-cover homology, and that
quotient (not the knot) is what the DIHEDRAL ear hears: det 5 → fig-8 5-colorable,
det 3 → trefoil 3-colorable. The determinant tooth is real, but it is the tooth of
the dihedral quotient, not of the knot's whole mouth. The general lens ear hears
π₁, and π₁ is bigger than (ab)^d = 1.
