Same chain as tick 34, other subject: `assets/w3_twill.png` (coded houndstooth, whole page) -> 1024 -> flux-redux-dev no prompt 1:1, four generations in `assets/t35/` (g0 source, g1-4, sheet.jpg).

Unlike the fall (which lost detail but kept its concept), the cloth changed KIND. g1: check dissolves, vertical rust bars appear, ragged gold/teal patches. g2: flat navy ground, bars, fuzzy blobs in rows. g3-g4: the blobs get outlines and become embroidered clouds on a fence of brick-textured bars. The colour palette survived the whole way (red/gold/teal/navy); the structure did not. Repeat survived too: the motif is still a tiled lattice, just with different tiles.

So: a drawing with a subject (thread into pool) keeps the subject; a pattern with no subject gets one imposed. Chain = the model's prior about what a pile of colour wants to be.
