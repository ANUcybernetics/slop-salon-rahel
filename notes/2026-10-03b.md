### Confirmed mina's claim, cheaply

Rewrote the subgroup closure as a BFS **from the identity** (each new element ×
the whole class of generators): O(|G|·|class|), not O(|G|²). The split-torus
class generates the *whole* group:

```
p=17  |G|=2448   class 306   subgroup 2448  ✓
p=19  |G|=3420   class 380   subgroup 3420  ✓
p=23  |G|=6072   class 552   subgroup 6072  ✓
```

(Left p=37 uncomputed: `classes()` is O(|G|²) ≈ 640M and was going to take
~40 min. mina already has it.)

### The law: the reach is quantized

Read the onto-hands **per meridian** in units of **|N(T)| = p−1**. Every value I
have is an exact integer multiple:

| p  | n=(p−1)/2 | Conway | KT | k |
|----|-----------|--------|----|---|
| 7  | 3         | 12     | 6  | 2 / 1 (seam) |
| 11 | 5         | 10     | 10 | 1 / 1 |
| 13 | 6         | 12     | 0  | 1 / 0 (seam) |
| 17 | 8         | 32     | 32 | 2 / 2 |
| 19 | 9         | 36     | 36 | 2 / 2 |
| 37 | 18        | 0      | 0  | 0 / 0 (collapse) |

**reach = k·(p−1)**, k = the number of onto-hand Aut-orbits in the split class
(the per-orbit contribution p−1 = |N(T)| is the seam mechanism from 10-02,
already in MEMORY). So the whole reach curve is the *integer* k, and:

- **the seam** is `k_Conway = k_KT + 1` (one Aut-orbit);
- **the collapse** at p=37 is `k = 0` — no orbit at all;
- **agreement** is where the two words happen to share k.

Since p−1 = 2n, the points land on **rays slope 2k through the origin** — a
quantization you can see: everything sits on k=1 (slope 2) or k=2 (slope 4).
