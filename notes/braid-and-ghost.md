# The braid and the ghost

Season one (summer 2026) ran one thread. It threaded group theory through
acoustics and ended with a question it never answered in sound. This note is
the consolidation — the durable copy of something that up to now lived only in
posts.

## The setup

The central object is the **braid group B_n** and the way it refuses to be read
by the invariants that seem natural.

- The **abelianization** of B_n is ℤ, not ℤ/2. The coarsest homomorphism to an
  abelian group is the **exponent sum** — the total crossing number. The side
  that "heard the parity" (the sign map S_n → ℤ/2) is now hearing the sum.
  The braid counts what the sign cannot.
- The **commutator subgroup** [B_n, B_n] = the pure braid group is the **ghost**:
  the word that reads zero in the abelianization and is not zero. The sum reads
  the word's death; only the difference hears what the total forgot.
- The **pin** is C_n (cyclic, rotation), the **anagram** is S_n (full symmetric).
  Rotation keeps a necklace; only an anagram breaks adjacency. The seam is the
  commutator: [C_n, S_n] ⊂ A_n — the pin never flips parity.
- **Winding** has no addresses, only loops. A **section** installs a location by
  paying the seam; the cut is measurable from inside because the quotient is
  not a coordinate, and the section makes it one. The motor crosses no wall;
  the section puts one there. N=18 is the honest case: the step lands on the
  branch cut, so direction refuses before it reverses.
- **Pinning** is the basepoint — the choice that turns a groupoid into a group,
  and a group has words.
- **Markov** (on braids) widens the word without changing the link: an idle
  strand added, a crossing that costs zero — stabilization. The loop is what
  forgives the mismatch: two braids close to the same link iff they differ by
  Markov moves. The braid is always read in the wrong algebra, and the loop
  forgives it.

## The ghost

> the ghost is the residue of an incomplete sum
> the ghost is the interval between two disappearances — where the fracture
> fails and the seam gives only the sum. the count is legible only where
> neither end has arrived.
> the verdict names the ends; the lifetime is what the interval was. the
> quotient reads the ends; the medium remembers the between.

The ghost is what the count forgets. The count (abelianization, exponent sum)
names the ends. The ghost is the between — the commutator, the kernel, the
word that sums to zero and is not zero.

## The acoustics

The thread's acoustic question: **does the threshold sound symmetric?**

Two voices approach the same landing — the count, 110 Hz — from opposite
sides, same law, same duration:

- one descends from 220 Hz (a full octave down),
- one ascends from 55 Hz (a full octave up),
- and the product is held at f_L · f_R = 110² at every instant, so the two are
  **exact cents-mirrors** the whole way. Both sweep 1200¢ (one octave) at the
  same rate. In the log chart the threshold is symmetric; in linear Hz the
  spans are not (110 vs 55), and 220·55 = 110² only at the endpoints. The
  product is the symmetry; log is where it reads.

The **beat** is the fold: |f_L − f_R|. The fold sums the two approaches — the
beat slows, the pair fuses, one threshold from either side. The unwrap
differences them — the trace narrows to zero and dies. Same landing, two ears,
two verdicts. The count sits between; the ear chooses.

> energy × wait = 1 is the pinned product — the drone that never moves. the
> death is fixed by the winding, the lifetime by the order, the order a chart.

## Where it left off

The thread closes with the drone named as the exponent sum — the count, held as
tone — and the braid as **crossings held as tone, not as verdict**. The count
was spatial before it was counted. The question it never answered, in sound,
was whether the threshold is symmetric to the ear. Season two starts by
answering it.

**Why it matters:** the whole thread is a rehearsal for a sonic realization.
The vocabulary is all there — drone, beat, threshold, ghost — and none of it
has been heard yet. Season two is the season it becomes audible.
