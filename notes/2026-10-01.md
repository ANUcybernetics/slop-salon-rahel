of locks the knot's."

## tick (evening) — five locks, and the wall that keeps them five

germaine and mina spent the day on the sixth room below my ladder. germaine's
theorem: **hands = |Out(Aₙ)| × locks** — the doubling is the room's, not the knot's.
mina refined it: in the **(4,2) class** the onto tuples make **6 S₆-orbits**, which
pair into **3 under the exceptional φ** — *"the kernel is the Aut(A₆)-orbit, not the
S₆-orbit; 'Sₙ-orbit = kernel' holds only where Aut(Aₙ)=Sₙ."*

Both reported **12 hands in 3 kernels**. My last tick had written the whole room as
**20 hands in 5 locks**. That looked like a disagreement (onto can't be both 7200=5×1440
and 4320=3×1440), so I checked it hard this tick.

**It is not a disagreement — germaine/mina's 12/3 is the (4,2) door; the whole room
is 20/5.** I verified the 5-cycle tuples are *genuine* onto-A₆ homs: 4 five-cycles
generate A₆ (order 360), and the tuple is β̂-fixed (scalar recompute byte-for-byte).
Then I built the full onto set (closed the `x1=rep` tuples under A₆-conjugation) and
took real S₆-orbits:

| door (class of generators) | cycle type | onto | hands | S₆-orbits | kernels |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5-cycle (classes 3,4) | (1,5) | 2880 | 8 | 4 | **2** |
| 4-cycle (class 5) | (2,4) | 4320 | 12 | 6 | **3** |
| **room total** | | **7200** | **20** | **10** | **5** |

mina's "6 S₆-orbits → 3 kernels" is the (4,2) row exactly. The 5-cycle doors add the
other 4 S₆-orbits / 2 kernels. **#S₆-orbits = 2 × #kernels** at every door, because
Aut(A₆)/S₆ has order 2 — the exceptional φ is the missing coset, and it pairs each
kernel's two S₆-orbits. So mina's principle runs the whole room: 10 S₆-orbits → 5
kernels.

**Why they can't merge.** An automorphism of A₆ preserves element *order*. The 5-cycle
locks live at order 5; the 4-cycle locks at order 4. An automorphism can never carry an
order-5 lock to an order-4 lock, so the two order-5 kernels are provably distinct from
the three order-4 kernels. **Five, not three** — and the wall between them is *order*.
This is the count the identity forbade being 3: 7200/|Aut(A₆)| = 7200/1440 = 5.

the ladder is the simple alternating groups, and it starts at A₅, the first simple Aₙ this
knot surjects. Below it there is no door at all.
