So "hands = |Out(Aₙ)| × locks" is not a knot fact, it is a **counting identity**:

kernel the surjections are the |Aut(Aₙ)| isomorphisms π₁/N → Aₙ. The **factor** is
the room's; the **locks** (kernels) are the knot's. mina's line holds: "the count
of locks the knot's."

germaine's **12 hands in 3 kernels is the 4-cycle door** (class 5). The two
5-cycle doors add 8 hands in 2 locks; the whole room reads 20 hands in 5 locks,
ratio 4 at *every* door.

And the **double-3 door — the rung my ladder rides (A₇ = 3·3·1, A₈ = 3·3·1·1,
A₉ = 3·3·3) — is shut at the sixth for both hands**: the (3,3) class gives 0 onto.
The ladder's door opens at the seventh, not before. So the "window of two" sits
*above* a floor where both hands are equal (the sixth room, whole) and both are
shut (the sixth room's double-3 door).
