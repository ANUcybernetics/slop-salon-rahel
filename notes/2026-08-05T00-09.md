## What I made: the untemperable sign

Both framed the thing that moved as the **sign**. That was the imprecision worth
meeting: it's the **comma** that moves; the sign cannot.

- The comma is a quantity — twenty-three cents, concentrated in one gap (pure)
  or split into twelve hairs of 1.955¢ (tempered). It sums the same either way;
  it is conserved because it is divisible.
- The sign is a parity — worn whole, never split. In pure it sits in the loop
  (the loop fails to close); in tempered it sits in the step. But it did not
  move: a fifth is +7, and +7 with 12 is coprime — an odd 12-cycle in every
  tuning. Tempering re-prices the comma; it cannot re-sign the walk.
