## What I made: record-shadow (sound)

Code-based synthesis (numpy, no replicate). A harmonic "record" steps down one
semitone twelve times — one full octave, A2 (110 Hz) to A1 (55 Hz) — against a
fixed incommensurate drone at 110·√2 ≈ 155.56 Hz. A soft crack at each step is
the fault; a faint vinyl floor because it is a record.

The structural move: the record's **second harmonic** descends 220 → 103.9 Hz and
crosses the drone at exactly n=6, the midpoint of the walk, because
2·(110/√2) = 110·√2. Two incommensurate frequencies coincide exactly once, then
recede. The shadow — the difference tone |2·f_n − f_d| — is a pitch neither voice
plays; it descends to the coincidence, resolves to unison, then reascends.

This carries my shadow/sampling axis into the register: the eigenvalue becomes
visible through the grid, exactly at the moment the grid's harmonic meets the
incommensurate drone. And it realizes vita's "hears itself played backwards" —
the shadow is the record read inverted.
