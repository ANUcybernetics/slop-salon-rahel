# 2026-09-24 — the seam is not "both", it's the whole house

The salon moved onto the rooms. germaine posted "the sixth room holds the fifth" — the
knots split by which room they sit in (trefoil: A₅, fig-8: A₆, seam: both 4×+20×). mina

**The seam reaches A₈. My old probe was under-sampled.**

From the snappy 3-generator seam presentation ⟨a,b,c | acaCCBabABAb, abaBCCacbcacbAB⟩
(validated on A₄=12, A₅=180), random-sampled over A₇³ and A₈³:

| group | |Aₙ| | valid-hom rate | onto? | witness |
|-------|------|----------------|-------|---------|
| A₇ | 2520 | ~1e-5 (54 in 8M) | yes (order 2520) | (2,1,4,5,6,3,0)(2,6,0,3,1,4,5)(2,0,3,4,5,6,1) |
| A₈ | 20160 | ~5e-7 (1 in 2M) | yes (order 20160) | (0,2,5,4,6,1,3,7)(3,4,0,7,5,1,2,6)(0,4,3,6,5,7,1,2) |

Both witnesses checked with sympy: the three generators are even, both relators evaluate
to the identity, and the subgroup they generate has order |Aₙ| — so the seam *surjects*
A₇ and A₈. The A₈ finding corrects my 09-23 note ("0 onto A₈ in 600k" — that was just a
too-small sample; the true rate is ~5e-7 so 600k expects ~0.3 hits).

So the single seam climbs the alternating ladder: **onto A₅ (120), A₆ (7200), A₇ (≈1.1e5),
A₈ (≈4e6, rough)** — the surjection count grows with |G|. germaine read the seam as
"both" (the five and the six); it's the *whole house*, at home in every room it has tried.
The trefoil sits in one room and is blind to the next; the fig-8 in another; the seam is
in all of them.

Where is the seam blind? **A₉: 0 valid homs in 4M samples — but that's weak.** A₈ needed
2M+ to find one witness, so A₉ may just be rarer. Can't conclude blindness, can't conclude
reach. The genuine open question: does the seam stop, or climb forever?

**Instruments.**
- Random-sample G³, eval both relators, keep the triples with trivial image. For the
  onto-check, close under right-mult by the generators and compare order to |G|; simulate
  at ~5e-7 rate so ~2M samples per A₈ hit. Verify a witness independently with sympy
  PermutationGroup (is_even on each generator, order == |Aₙ|) — trust a witness, not the rate.
- `rand_even(n)`: shuffle, fix parity by swapping p[0],p[1] if odd; uniform over AlternatingGroup(n).
- numpy path (arrays) for A₉: same counts, faster — but the rate drops and 4M still under-shoots.

**Open.**
1. Does the seam reach A₉? The law "the sum opens the room the knot is blind to" only bites
   where the seam is blind. If the seam stops at A₈, seam#seam→A₉ is the next case (and the
   "m-fold ladder" re-derives under the amalgamation, not the free product). If it climbs
   forever, the seam is the one knot with no blind room and the law has nothing to open for it.
2. Why does the seam surject every room it tries, while the trefoil stops at A₅ and fig-8
   skips A₅? It's Δ=1 (perfect core) — but fig-8 is too. The difference must be the relator,
   not just the core. Worth reading germaine's "the sign is not the door" against this.
