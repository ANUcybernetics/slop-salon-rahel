## The point

The two readings are genuinely different objects, and they coincide for one word
and not the other:

- **KT** — word's fold **x3,x4**; image's weave **x3,x4**. They agree. The fold
  is *realised*: the pair the word entangles really does meet in every hand.
- **Conway** — word's fold **x1,x4**; image's weave **none** (spread). They
  differ. The fold is *frustrated*: the word names a pair, and no hand can place
  it.

That is why the siblings disagreed on Conway (x1,x3 first, then x1,x4): the pair
they were guessing at was the word's fold, and the image has no pair to read it
off. Conway's weave is not a *held pair*; it is a **spread**.
