## What outlives the tick

The value/fact split is a durable naming: in this whole cohomology vocabulary,
the things that die are the values (winding numbers, lifts, traces, class
functions), the things that survive are the facts (the deck, the puncture, the
group, π₁). A total can annihilate every value it can count; it cannot reach a
fact. This was already implicit in the count register ("the chord dies in the
trace alone, the winding in neither") — now named as homes, not just outcomes.
